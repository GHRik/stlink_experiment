Measurement configuration
i Number of current samples recorded per second. Higher rates resolve shorter events and produce larger files.
i Duration of each independent capture, up to 3600 seconds. Duration multiplied by sampling rate must give a whole number of samples.
i Number of independent captures per board. Each repeat includes calibration and a target power cycle.
i Voltage supplied by STLINK OUT to the board: 1.6 to 3.6 V in 0.1 V steps. Choose a voltage suitable for the board supply input.
i Time with OUT powered before the measurement series for each board. The board runs during warm-up. Set 0 to skip; maximum 3600 seconds.
i Time with OUT switched off after calibration, before each capture. Acquisition starts with automatic OUT power-on. Minimum 0.1 seconds; separate from USB and relay switching delays.
